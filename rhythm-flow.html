Rhythmanalysis: map existing rhythms · Rhythmactivation: design proposed interventions
Rhythmanalysis Observe & document the existing rhythms of this place. Trace how geography, morphology, qualities, and time connect in what you perceive right now. Add rhythms you identify, then draw connections across the four columns.
Rhythmactivation Design proposed interventions to shift or strengthen rhythms. Your analysis rhythms remain as a baseline. Add proposed rhythms (shown as dashed lines) to explore what could be different. What new connections would change the character of this place?
